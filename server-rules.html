Sony/Microsoft Tracker Rules

Notice: By joining this server you allow Wingman to log everything you send to train the AI model. Information can be removed at any time with /remove.

1. Be Respectful

Treat all members with respect and kindness. Avoid hate speech, discrimination, harassment, or any form of bullying.

2. No NSFW Content

Do not share or discuss explicit, sexual, or adult content. This includes text, images, links, or discussions.

3. Keep It Safe

Do not share personal information, including addresses, phone numbers, or other private details. Protect your own privacy and the privacy of others.

4. No Spamming

Avoid excessive self-promotion, spamming, or flooding the chat with repetitive messages.

5. No Trolling or Flamebait

Do not intentionally provoke or incite arguments. Constructive criticism is encouraged, but trolling or flamebaiting will not be tolerated.

6. Respect Staff Decisions

Follow the instructions and decisions of the server's moderators and administrators. If you have concerns, address them privately, such as in a ticket.

7. English Preferred

While we welcome diversity, we primarily communicate in English to ensure everyone can understand and participate.

8. No Advertising

Do not promote other Discord servers, products, or services in different channels.

9. Keep Voice Channels Tidy

In voice channels, avoid excessive background noise, loud music, or disruptions that interfere with conversations.

10. Reporting Issues

If you encounter a problem or observe a rule violation, report it to the moderators via direct message or by making a ticket.

11. No Alternate (Alt) Accounts

Do not use multiple accounts on the server. Each member should have one account.

12. Follow Discord Guidelines

Always follow Discord's own guidelines: Discord Community Guidelines

13. No Refunds

Strictly as stated, we do have a small refund policy under some purchases.

14. No Leaking Codes

No leaking codes from any other tracker. Codes can be shared with 1–2 friends using Discord's forwarding feature.

15. NO 67 (Six-Seven)

Strictly no 67 (Six-Seven), as it is repetitive and annoying.
